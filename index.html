Our Giving Promise

Supporting Hunger at Home in San Jose

Our model is simple: 100% of plant revenue is directed to Hunger at Home, an authorized 501(c)(3) nonprofit distributing fresh, nutritious meals across Silicon Valley.

The garden's operating costs are covered personally, so every dollar you contribute goes straight to hunger relief.
