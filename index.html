Our Giving Promise

Supporting Hunger at Home in San Jose

Nourishing Our Neighbors: In a valley filled with innovation, it is heartbreaking that 1 in 4 of our neighbors quietly wonders where their next meal will come from.

Hunger at Home steps into that gap with immense compassion, ensuring over 75% of their weekly grocery distributions reach families with young children and seniors.

Hunger at Home recovers surplus food from local businesses and works with nonprofit partners to distribute meals to people facing food insecurity. The organization reports 14.4 million meals distributed through its food rescue work and partners.
